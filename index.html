A practice to try

Between work and the rest of your day.

Before you reach for your keys or open the next app, feel where your feet meet the floor. Let one breath come and go at its own pace. Notice what part of the day you’re still carrying.

A question for the pause

“What do I want to bring into the next part of my day?”

You might choose a word, stretch your shoulders, or simply sit for another breath.

Meet your coach

Kristin Schutz

I have a master’s degree in applied psychology and over 20 years of teaching experience. I’m trained in Hatha, Vinyasa, and Kundalini yoga, and my coaching has taken me from college teams to executive and government settings.

What I love is bringing those worlds together. We can talk about a pattern you recognize, then get out of our heads for a moment and explore what changes when you move, breathe, or let yourself be still.
